## Part A — Proportionality & Proportion Coefficient (20 pts)

**Question 1.** (4 pts) The table below shows the distance traveled by a car at constant speed.

| Time (hours) | 2 | 3 | 5 | 7 |
|--------------|---|---|---|---|
| Distance (km) | 70 | 105 | 175 | 245 |

a) Is this a proportional relationship? Justify your answer.
b) Determine the **proportion coefficient** `k`.
c) Write a formula relating distance `d` to time `t`.

**Question 2.** (4 pts) The table below is proportional with coefficient `k = 3`.
Complete the missing values:

| x | 2 | 4 | ?  | 10 |
|---|---|---|----|----|
| y | 6 | ? | 21 | ?  |

**Question 3.** (4 pts) Solve for `x` using cross-multiplication:

$$\frac{4}{10} = \frac{x}{25}$$

**Question 4.** (4 pts) A recipe uses **150 g of flour** for **4 cookies**.
How many grams of flour are needed for **18 cookies**?

**Table b)**

# Answer Key (Teacher's Copy)

> All numerical answers below were verified symbolically with SymPy.

### Part A

**1.** a) Yes — every ratio `distance / time = 35`, constant.
   b) `k = 35` (km/h).
   c) `d = 35 · t`.

**2.** With `k = 3`: `y = 3x`.
   - `x = 4 → y = 12`
   - `y = 21 → x = 21 / 3 = 7`
   - `x = 10 → y = 30`
   So the completed table is **x: 2, 4, 7, 10** and **y: 6, 12, 21, 30**.

**3.** `x = (4 · 25) / 10 = 100 / 10 = ` **10**.

**4.** Coefficient `k = 150 / 4 = 37.5` g per cookie.
   For 18 cookies: `18 · 37.5 = ` **675 g**.

**5.** a) **Proportional** — every ratio `y / x = 3` is constant (k = 3).
   b) **Not proportional** — ratios are `3/1 = 3`, `4/2 = 2`, `5/3`, `6/4 = 1.5` — not constant.
   (In fact `y = x + 2`, which is an *additive*, not multiplicative, relation.)

### Part B

**6.** `25/100 · 80 = ` **20**.

**7.** `15 / 60 = 1/4 = ` **25 %**.

**8.** `200 · (1 + 12/100) = 200 · 1.12 = ` **224 €**.

**9.** `50 · (1 − 20/100) = 50 · 0.80 = ` **40 €**.

**10.** `30/100 · 250 = ` **75 students**.

### Part C

**11.** Degree = **2**, leading coefficient = **3**.

**12.** `P(x) + Q(x) = ` **`4x² − 3x + 3`**.

**13.** `P(x) − Q(x) = ` **`2x² + 7x − 5`**.

**14.** `2x · (x + 3) = ` **`2x² + 6x`**.

**15.** `(x + 4)(x − 2) = ` **`x² + 2x − 8`**.

**16.** `P(2) = 4 − 6 + 5 = ` **3**.

### Bonus

`80 · 0.75 = 60`, then `60 · 1.25 = 75`. Final price is **75 €**, **not** 80 €.
Reason: the 25 % increase is applied to the *reduced* base (60), not the original 80,
so successive equal % changes do **not** cancel out.
